# Wellness Water Company — Social Creative Kit V2 (Fresh Concepts)
## 15 New Static Posts + 5 New Carousels — with an Image Prompt for Every Frame

*A completely new creative direction built around real American life: the morning coffee, the cooler at the cookout, the dog's water bowl, the $200 skincare routine, the family well, and the homeowner's instinct to protect what's theirs. No recycled "scary glass of water" tropes. Every concept connects an everyday moment to the product — and to one action: book a free in-home water test.*

---

## CREATIVE PRINCIPLES (what makes this set different)

1. **Lead with the ritual, not the contaminant.** People don't think about "TDS" — they think about their coffee, their kid's bottle, their game-day ice. We hijack those moments.
2. **Use the unexpected hero.** The villain isn't always a molecule — sometimes it's a *receipt*, an *ice cube*, a *vanity full of products*, or a *40-year-old well*.
3. **Talk like a neighbor, not a lab.** Plain, confident, a little wit. American self-reliance: *"You can't fix the city. You can fix your tap."*
4. **Always resolve the tension** with the free test + the fix (softener / whole-house / RO / lifetime warranty).

**Visual system (carry over from the brand):** navy `#1F3864`, ocean blue `#2E74B5`, bright aqua `#35C6E6` accent, crisp white, warm lifestyle tones where the concept is human/cozy; alert amber only for "cost/problem" beats. Bold caps headlines (Poppins/Gilroy Black), clean body (Inter). Locked logo + aqua **"FREE Water Test →"** chip. Formats: 1:1 (1080×1080) and 4:5 (1080×1350); 9:16 for stories.

# PRODUCTION & USAGE NOTES

- **Headlines do the stopping; images earn the second look.** Keep headline text huge and legible at thumbnail size.
- **One CTA, everywhere:** "FREE Water Test." Lowest friction, highest conversion.
- **Localize for resonance:** swap settings/words for the brand's markets (e.g., a Phoenix backyard, a Florida porch, a Texas ranch well) and city names in headlines.
- **Keep people authentic:** cast/style models to reflect real American customers across ages and regions; avoid stocky, sterile imagery.
- **Rotate the emotional beats** so the feed stays fresh: ritual → money → beauty → heartland → self-reliance.
- **Carousel best-bets to post first:** #3 (Hard Water Tax invoice) and #4 (products vs. water) tend to earn the most saves and shares.
- **Compliance:** keep cost/stat claims qualitative or localized and cited; add the physician-review stamp on skin/health beats; add alt text on every frame.
