Issues & Opportunities

Identified Problems & Growth Potential

Current Issues to Address

Posting Consistency

Irregular posting schedule reduces algorithm favorability and audience retention. Need consistent 2-3 posts per week minimum.

Content Variety

Limited content types (mostly static posts). Missing video content, carousels, stories which drive higher engagement.

Engagement Rate

Low interaction with followers. Need to respond to comments, DMs, and actively engage with community.

Profile Optimization

Bio, highlights, and link-in-bio need refreshing to clearly communicate value proposition and drive action.

Growth Opportunities

Local Story Angle

Only true local Calgary manufacturer - massive opportunity to own "Made in Calgary" narrative and tap into local pride.

Video Content Gap

Competitors underutilizing video. Factory tours, installation time-lapses, and team content can differentiate Apollo.

Customer Stories

25+ years means thousands of happy customers. Untapped resource for testimonials, reviews, and UGC content.

Seasonal Content

Calgary's extreme weather creates natural content opportunities around energy savings, winter prep, and home comfort.
