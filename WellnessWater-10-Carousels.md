## QUICK-USE NOTES

- **Format:** 1:1 (1080×1080) or 4:5 (1080×1350). 6–8 slides each.
- **Rule of thumb:** one idea per slide, short headline, big legible type, consistent footer (logo + `wellnesswatercompany.com`).
- **Visual system:** navy `#1F3864`, ocean blue `#2E74B5`, bright aqua `#35C6E6` accent, crisp white; warm lifestyle tones for human concepts; alert amber only on "problem/cost" beats. Headlines in bold caps (Poppins/Gilroy Black), body in Inter.
- **Locked CTA:** aqua **"FREE Water Test →"** button on the final slide.
- **Cover slides** always carry a "Swipe →" cue and the hardest-hitting hook.
