# Wellness Water Company — Social Media Creative Kit
## 10 Static Posts + 4 Carousels (Copy + Art Direction + Image Prompts)

*Scroll-stopping, conversion-focused creative for Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Every concept ladders up to one action: book a free in-home water test. Image prompts are written for both a human designer and AI tools (Midjourney / DALL·E / Firefly).*

---

## BRAND CREATIVE SYSTEM (apply to every post)

**Color palette**
- Deep navy `#1F3864` (authority, trust) — primary background
- Ocean blue `#2E74B5` / `#4472C4` (brand blue)
- Bright aqua/cyan `#35C6E6` (energy, "clean water" pop / accent + CTA)
- Crisp white `#FFFFFF` + soft mist gray `#F2F6F9`
- Alert amber `#F5A623` — use ONLY on "threat/problem" posts for tension

**Typography**
- Headlines: bold geometric sans (Montserrat / Poppins / Gilroy — ExtraBold/Black), tight tracking, ALL CAPS
- Subtext: clean sans (Inter / Open Sans — Regular/Medium)

**Locked elements (on every creative)**
- Logo: small, top-left or bottom-center
- CTA chip (rounded, aqua): **"FREE Water Test →"**
- Handle/site: `wellnesswatercompany.com`
- Optional trust badges to rotate: *NASA-Grade Filtration · Lifetime Warranty · Physician-Backed · Free Water Test*

**Tone**
Confident, clear, human. Hard-hitting on problems, warm and reassuring on solutions. Never fear-mongering for its own sake — always pair a problem with the fix.

**Formats**
- Feed: 1:1 (1080×1080) and 4:5 (1080×1350, more reach)
- Stories/Reels covers: 9:16 (1080×1920)

# POSTING & PERFORMANCE NOTES

- **Hook in the first 3 words.** The headline does the heavy lifting; the image earns the stop.
- **Always pair problem + fix.** Never leave the audience anxious without the solution + free test.
- **One CTA everywhere:** "FREE Water Test." It's the lowest-friction, highest-converting ask.
- **Static cadence:** rotate fear → benefit → offer → trust so the feed isn't all doom or all sales.
- **Carousels** earn the most saves/shares — post the checklist (Carousel 4) and the cost one (Carousel 2) first.
- **Localize for reach:** swap headlines/subtext with city names for the brand's markets (e.g., "PHOENIX, YOUR WATER IS HARD" / "IS LA TAP WATER SAFE?") to tap local relevance.
- **Accessibility:** keep text high-contrast, add alt text, and caption any video/Reel versions.
