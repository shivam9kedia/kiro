### The Strategy

Forget 100K+ influencers (Rs 10,000-50,000 per post). Target **3K-15K follower micro-influencers** in your exact niche.

### Why This Works

- 60-80% of micro-influencers accept FREE PRODUCT for a Reel
- Their audience trusts them more than celebrities
- Indian Gen Z buys what their "relatable big sister" recommends
- Cost per influencer post: Rs 300 (cost of free product)

### Your Action Plan

```
WEEK 1: BUILD LIST OF 50 INFLUENCERS
├── Search Instagram: #korean #kbeauty #hairaccessories #softgirl
├── Filter: 3K-15K followers, India-based, regular posters
├── Specifically target: college students, lifestyle micros, hair vloggers

WEEK 2: OUTREACH (30 DMs/day, 5 days)
Template:
"Hi [name]! I love your content (especially the one about [specific reel]).
I run a small Korean-aesthetic scrunchie brand and would love to send you
our premium set as a gift — no obligations, but if you love them, I'd
appreciate a Reel/story tag. Drop your address?"

WEEK 3: SHIP TO 10-15 WHO RESPOND
WEEK 4: 8-12 WILL POST (typical conversion)

EXPECTED RESULT:
├── 8-12 micro-influencer posts x 5K avg followers = 40K-60K reach
├── At 1% conversion rate = 400-600 profile visits
├── 10-30 DMs from these visits
├── 3-10 orders directly attributable
├── Your cost: Rs 3,000-5,000 in free products
└── Equivalent ad spend value: Rs 15,000-25,000 worth of reach
```

### Pro Tips:
- Send the product BEAUTIFULLY packaged (their unboxing = free ad)
- Include a handwritten note (creates emotional reaction = better content)
- Don't demand the post — let them feel free
- Repost their content on your page (free content for you)

### The Strategy

Instead of running ads to all of India, OWN 1-2 cities first.

### Why This Works

- Lower CPM in concentrated targeting
- Word-of-mouth spreads faster
- Easier to do offline events (college, flea markets)
- Hashtag domination becomes possible (#mumbaifashion #delhihair etc.)
- Local micro-influencers stronger relationships

### Action Plan

**Pick ONE city (your home city ideal):**
- Mumbai
- Delhi
- Bangalore
- Pune
- Hyderabad

**Then:**
1. Run ads ONLY to that city (lower competition = lower costs)
2. Target college campuses by name (DU colleges, Mumbai University, etc.)
3. Engage with city-specific hashtags daily
4. Post stories tagged with your city
5. Once you dominate locally, expand

**Expected impact:** 30-50% lower CPM, 2-3x higher engagement rates

## TACTIC 7: REVERSE PSYCHOLOGY — POSITION AS LIMITED/ARTISANAL

### The Strategy

Cheap competitors say: "Buy now! Lots in stock! Mass produced!"
You say: "Only 30 made this batch. Once gone, gone."

### Why This Works

- Scarcity drives FOMO (Instagram's secret weapon)
- "Limited" = "Premium" psychology
- Forces faster decisions (less "I'll think about it")
- Creates collector mentality

### Implementation

```
ALWAYS use language like:
- "Only 30 sets in this batch — limited stock"
- "Restocking next week, this color was only 20 pieces"
- "Sold out yesterday in 2 hours"
- "Drop closes Sunday midnight"

NEVER use:
- "Always available"
- "Mass stock"
- "Bulk discount"
```

### Drop-Style Releases (Like Streetwear)

Instead of "always available":
- Launch new colors as DROPS every 2-3 weeks
- Build hype with countdown stories
- Sell out the batch fast
- Restocking = excitement event

This creates **brand mystique** that Rs 100-Meesho-scrunchies can never have.

## PART 5: TOUCHING YOUR CUSTOMER (DIRECTLY)

### The 7-Touch Customer Journey

Most sellers do 1-2 touches. Premium brands do 7+. Here's your blueprint:

```
TOUCH 1: Discovery (Reel/Ad)
└── Customer scrolls past, hooked by your aesthetic

TOUCH 2: Profile Visit
└── They see your highlights, polished feed, story polls

TOUCH 3: Engagement (Story/Poll)
└── They reply to a story poll, you save them as warm lead

TOUCH 4: First DM
└── Personal welcome (not bot), voice note if possible

TOUCH 5: Order Confirmation
└── Personalized thank-you message + WhatsApp invite

TOUCH 6: Packaging Experience
└── Beautiful unboxing, handwritten note, surprise inclusion

TOUCH 7: Post-Delivery Follow-Up
└── Personal "how do you like them?" message, ask for review

TOUCH 8+: Community Inclusion
└── WhatsApp VIP group, exclusive drops, birthday wishes
```

### Each Touch Has a Purpose

| Touch # | Purpose | Cost | Impact |
|---------|---------|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Get attention | Time | Awareness |
| 3-4 | Build trust | Time | Conversion |
| 5-6 | Create wow | Rs 15-30 packaging | Loyalty |
| 7+ | Build community | Time | Repeat sales |

### Hidden Power: Voice Notes

Indian customers LOVE voice notes from sellers. It feels personal.

```
SAMPLE VOICE NOTE TEMPLATES:

For new DM:
"Hi [name]! Thanks SO much for messaging us. Just sent you our
catalog — let me know which set you like, I'll keep it aside for you!"

After payment received:
"Thank you so much [name] for ordering! Your set is being packed
right now with so much love. Will share tracking by tomorrow!"

After delivery:
"Hi [name]! Just checking in — did your scrunchies arrive?
Would love to hear what you think, and please feel free to tag
us if you take any pics!"
```

Cost: 30 seconds per customer. Impact: 10x trust building.
